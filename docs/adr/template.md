# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD (when the decision reached `main`, or when it was proposed)
- **Evidence:** PRs, files, measurements

## Context

What problem or constraint forced a choice. Facts only, with evidence.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two short paragraphs.

## Alternatives considered

- **Alternative:** why it was not chosen.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what is now a known limitation.
